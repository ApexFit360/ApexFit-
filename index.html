About

ApexFit

ApexFit is your daily fitness companion: simple workout plans, step-by-step yoga, a calorie guide for Indian food and reminders that keep you on track. Small steps, done every day, build a stronger you.

Fitness basics

🏃 Move

Aim for about 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, plus 2 days of strength training.

🥗 Nourish

Build meals around protein, fibre and vegetables. Go easy on fried and sugary foods.

💧 Hydrate

Sip water through the day. Pale-yellow urine is a simple sign you are well hydrated.

😴 Recover

Adults generally need 7–9 hours of sleep. Muscles grow during rest, so keep rest days.

Beginner rules

1. Start small and build up gradually.

2. Warm up for 5 minutes, cool down and stretch after.

3. Focus on form before speed or weight.

4. Stop if you feel sharp pain or dizziness.

BMI & daily calories

ApexFit shares general fitness information, not medical advice. Please consult a doctor before starting a new programme, especially if you have a health condition.
